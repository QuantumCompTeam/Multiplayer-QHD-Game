## Answer

Portfolio writeup for 'Entangled Equilibria': quantum game theory project extending quantum Nash equilibria from 2-player to N-player Hawk-Dove financial trading games via entanglement topologies (GHZ, W, ring, star, fully-connected). Uses EWL protocol in Qiskit. Key result: Q_N=U(0,pi/N,pi/N); for N=3, mutual Q is unique pure Nash at 4/3/player vs classical 1/3, advantage 1.0. Quantum advantage = quantum NE payoff minus classical NE payoff. Applications: carbon trading, market microstructure, DeFi. Stack: Qiskit, Qiskit Aer, NetworkX, NumPy/SciPy, Matplotlib.
