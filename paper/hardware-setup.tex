\subsection{Experimental Setup and Safeguards}
Every submission passes three gates: (i) an $\mathrm{Operator.equiv}$
circuit-identity assertion against the dense
$J^\dagger (U^{\otimes N}) J$ reference, (ii) a noiseless simulator dry-run
that must reproduce the exact analytic advantage, and (iii) a full rehearsal
of the batch, including mitigation analysis, on the device noise model reduced
to the executed qubits. The execution
configuration of each run family is summarized in Table~\ref{tab:hwconfig}.
